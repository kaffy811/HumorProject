# Sidequest: campus moments, better punchlines

## Audience and choices

Sam is a Columbia junior from the Midwest, discovering New York and collecting campus/city moments. The app turns one actual photo into four distinct captions. Sam compares four jokes, casts one honest vote per caption and shares a chosen caption with a group chat. No fake vote counters or fabricated feed results.

Reasons to return: a weekday photo prompt, a newest-first real member feed, and a Needs your vote filter. Reasons to spread content: a Share caption control uses native sharing when available and copies text otherwise. A shared caption includes a public homepage link; private image links, user identity and signed storage URLs are never copied.

Compared with an unstructured endless swipe session, four alternatives on the same actual image make judging humor easier. A saved vote survives refresh and cannot be duplicated. My photos helps users find their own posts. Profile details and generation audit records stay private. Mobile navigation, clear empty states, upload previews, truthful error states and a Privacy page support first-time visitors.
